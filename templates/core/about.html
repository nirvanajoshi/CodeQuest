About CodeQuest

The platform

CodeQuest is a gamified programming education platform where students learn through coding challenges, quizzes, and competitions — earning XP, badges, and streaks along the way.

How it works

Solve challenges to earn points, take quizzes to test your understanding, climb the leaderboard, and compete in scheduled competitions against other learners.
